Flight

The Flight Frame is worn in the chest slot. While it has power and a pilot it can carry, a model that passes Pilot, its wearer flies as freely as in creative play: jump twice to take off, and it hovers wherever you stop. It spends 80 FE a tick while you fly, so a full frame of 400,000 FE flies for about four minutes. The Fabricator makes it from a transformer photomask.

The pilot

A pilot goes in as an item goes into a bundle: in your inventory, pick up a checkpoint and right-click the frame with it. Right-click the frame with nothing in hand to take the pilot out again, back on its checkpoint. The frame's tooltip names its pilot, and says when it cannot hold the frame: a pilot has to pass Pilot, and be small enough to carry.

Power

Use the frame on an Energy Cell to fill it from the cell's charge. The bar on the frame shows how much it holds.

Learning to fly

A model learns to pilot on the Flight Dataset, by flying: the Core flies the model's own flights and it learns from how each choice turned out, as for Control. Thrust changes how fast the frame climbs or sinks, not where it is, so a pilot has to read its speed from the heights before; and the wind its vane reads pushes every two or three looks from a moment of its own, which it has to make out from the flight and meet before it pushes. A model that sees only the last few characters holds under half of the flights in its worst wind; a two-block transformer holds them all after about a thousand steps. See Models and Tests.
