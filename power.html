Power

Power moves in Forge Energy (FE) along Power Cables: from the blocks that make it to the blocks that store or use it, and to any other mod's block that takes FE.

Making it

The Combustion Generator burns what a furnace burns and makes 80 FE a tick while it burns. A fuel lasts half as long as in a furnace, so a coal makes 64,000 FE. It burns only while it has room for what it makes, and holds 32,000 FE of its own.

Crafting

Moving and storing it

Power Cables join generators, cells and machines into one network. Each tick the machines take what they need from the generators, then from the cells; whatever the generators make beyond that charges the cells. An Energy Cell holds 1,000,000 FE and moves up to 2,000 FE a tick each way. Its faces fill in quarters as it charges, and a comparator beside it reads how full it is.

The Smelter

The Smelter smelts anything a furnace does on 80 FE a tick instead of fuel, an item every five seconds. From an ore or a raw ore it makes two of what a furnace makes from one. The Fabricator makes it from a transformer photomask.

The controller

Put a model in the Smelter's Controller slot, and each ore becomes a melt it holds in its band: at a look every four ticks it asks the model whether to cool the melt, hold it or heat it, and waits for the answer. A melt held in the band at 90% of its looks or more makes three ingots instead of two; a model passes the Control test by holding nine melts in ten of every ore the test plays. The slot is in the panel beside the Smelter's screen; between its ore and its ingots, the screen draws the melt look by look, the band shaded, and pointing at it tells how much of the last melt was held. A model learns this on the Process Dataset: see Models and Tests.

Other blocks

Hoppers and Ports feed the generator and the Smelter from any side and take the Smelter's ingots out. Other mods' cables can draw power from generators and cells, and give it to cells and the Smelter.
