Power

Power moves in Forge Energy (FE) along Power Cables: from the blocks that make it to the blocks that store or use it, and to any other mod's block that takes FE.

A Combustion Generator burns what a furnace burns.

Power Cables lead away from it.

An Energy Cell on the cables keeps what is not used yet.

A Smelter on the cables works on what they bring.

The screens of power, part by part.

Making it

The Combustion Generator burns what a furnace burns and makes 80 FE a tick while it burns. A fuel lasts half as long as in a furnace, so a coal makes 64,000 FE. It burns only while it has room for what it makes, and holds 32,000 FE of its own.

Crafting

Steam

A Boiler with water touching it raises steam for the Turbines stacked on top of it, up to four. Each Turbine makes 240 FE a tick, and the Boiler burns a tick of a furnace's fuel for each one, so a coal makes 384,000 FE however many there are: six times what it makes in a Combustion Generator. More Turbines only burn it faster. Power comes out of the Boiler and the Turbines alike, and they spin and let their steam go while it burns.

The sun

A Solar Panel makes 40 FE a tick under a bright sky, less as it darkens toward night or rain, and nothing in the dark or under a roof. It costs nothing to run. Panels touching each other share a network with no cable between them, and cells carry what they make through the night.

Crafting

The reactor

The most power there is comes from a reactor, and only a model can run one.

Moving and storing it

Cables join generators, cells and machines into one network. Each tick the machines take what they need from the generators, then from the cells; whatever the generators make beyond that charges the cells. All of it goes through the cables, and in a tick a network carries no more than its thinnest cable does: one thin cable holds back the whole line. Blocks that touch need no cable and hold nothing back.

While a network carries power it runs along the cables as pulses of light.

An Energy Cell holds 1,000,000 FE and moves up to 2,000 FE a tick each way, and a Capacitor Bank holds 64,000,000 FE and moves up to 64,000 FE. Their faces fill in quarters as they charge, and a comparator beside one reads how full it is.

The Smelter

The Smelter smelts anything a furnace does on 80 FE a tick instead of fuel, an item every five seconds. From an ore or a raw ore it makes two of what a furnace makes from one. The Fabricator makes it from a transformer photomask.

The controller

Put a model in the Smelter's Controller slot, and each ore becomes a melt it holds in its band: at a look every four ticks it asks the model whether to cool the melt, hold it or heat it, and waits for the answer. A melt held in the band at 90% of its looks or more makes three ingots instead of two; a model passes the Control test by holding nine melts in ten of every ore the test plays. The slot is in the panel beside the Smelter's screen; between its ore and its ingots, the screen draws the melt look by look, the band shaded, and pointing at it tells how much of the last melt was held. A model learns this on the Process Dataset: see Models and Tests.

A controller that passes Precision holds each melt not in the band but at one step of it, and a melt held there at 70% of its looks makes six. No dataset teaches that: see Tuning. One that also passes Reckoning is handed an assay off each such melt, a line of numbers to add up, and if it gets that right the melt makes nine: see Thinking Longer.

Other blocks

Hoppers and Ports feed the generators and the Smelter from any side and take the Smelter's ingots out. Other mods' cables can draw power from generators, cells and the reactor, and give it to cells, Chillers and the Smelter.

Compute runs on power too, past the first few Accelerators: see Compute.
