# Ruins

The people who lived here before left their places behind: towers along the old roads, farms on the ridges, houses on
the marshes, slips on the shores, and stranger halls. Every kind of ruin is rare, some rarer than others, and each
stands only in the land that suits it: it takes travel to come across one, and a Wayfinder to find one on purpose.

## Finding them

A <ItemLink id="wayfinder" /> leads to ruins the archive has told you of. Use it to choose a kind to look for: it lists
the relay towers, which everyone knows of, and every kind a shard you have read tells of; read a shard, by using it,
and the places it writes about join the list. Its needle then points to the nearest ruin of that kind within 4,000
blocks, and while you hold it, it says how far that is.

## Relay towers

Stone towers banded at each floor, the tallest thing for half a day's walk, with four rooms one on top of another: the
store, the kitchen, the keeper's room, and at the top the lamp room, its great lamp of glass and redstone ringed with
copper, a railed walk round it and a roof of copper gone green. At the tower's foot stands the relay house: the
keeper's desk with the tower book on its lectern, and the stand where a big mind stood, ringed by accelerators in their
cooling water. In grassland, forest, taiga, snow and hills, at most one in each region 48 chunks across.

## Waymarks

Stone pillars by stretches of the old road, a torch on top and little stones left at the foot; one in four stands
under a little roof with notes left on it. The commonest ruin: at most one in each region 24 chunks across.

## Inns

The inns of the Long Road, a day apart: stone below and an oak frame above, the upper floor jutting over the road.
Inside, the hearth still burns; the riddle board hangs by the fire, the waymark book lies behind the counter next to a
pie, and the beds wait upstairs; the stable stands across the yard and the inn's sign by the road. At most one in each
region 64 chunks across.

## Windmill farms

The farms of the ridges: a windmill with its tally board by the door, the barn and the pig pen, a well in the yard, and
three fields, wheat, beetroot, and potatoes and carrots, each with its ditch, its hedge and its scarecrow or hives. On
plains, meadows, savannas and windswept hills, at most one in each region 56 chunks across.

## Stilt houses

A healer's house of mud brick on a deck on stilts over a marsh, its remedies on the bench, with a boardwalk to the
bank. In swamps and mangrove swamps, at most one in each region 48 chunks across.

## Slipways

A stone quay with a shipwright's shed and stacked timber, and running out over the sea the frame of a ship never
finished, a crane leaning over it. Only where a beach or a stony shore meets deep enough water, the slip toward the
sea: at most one in each region 40 chunks across.

## Tutor houses

Halls of tuff under a cornice of copper, a relay on the roof, where minds were drilled and tested. Apprentices still
stand in their row before the tutor's Bench; the teaching room keeps its tray of crystals, each on its card; the
apprentice hall keeps its dry box; and the drill yard, ringed with torches, its boards drawn as small fields. In
grassland, forest, taiga, savanna and cherry groves, at most one in each region 72 chunks across.

## Fallen relay masts

Where a relay mast fell it broke a bowl in the ground, grassed over now at the rim. The mast lies across it in three
pieces of green copper, its relay half buried, and its drive housing split open: the chest inside holds
<ItemLink id="archive_drive" />s already filled with the relays' writing. At most one in each region 40 chunks across.

## Sealed stacks

Deep under the ground, with no way in but a wall of clay: stacks of the archive sealed in its last days, shelves
behind wax and the archive's seal, boxes of records in waxed cloth, the sealing book on its lectern, and a lamp left on
the long table. Their boxes hold filled <ItemLink id="archive_drive" />s. At most one in each region 64 chunks across.

## Archive houses, relay masts and buried vaults

Small houses of calcite and tuff, one corner usually fallen in, that kept records; tall copper masts with an old relay
still on top; and, under a lantern on a post beside a trapdoor, deep rooms with a bank of old accelerators and two
chests, every one holding a [salvaged model](salvage.md). At most one in each region 56 chunks across, the vaults 64.

## What they hold

- <ItemLink id="archive_shard" />: pieces of the archive, mostly of what was written about the place they lie in: a
  relay tower's shards are mostly its keepers' writing. See [The Archive](archive.md).
- Photomasks with salvaged designs: the Fabricator makes working but unfused machines from them, without a Bench.
  Linear layer, softmax and GELU masks turn up in houses, matrix multiply ones under the gravel, and attention, layer
  norm, feed-forward and transformer masks in vaults. See [The Bench and the Fabricator](bench.md).
- <ItemLink id="schematic_relays" />: the plans for relays, a pattern to copy that stays in the crafting grid. In
  relay towers, on masts and in vaults.
- What each place kept: coal and food in the towers, flour and seed on the farms, remedies in the marsh, timber and
  tools at the slips; <ItemLink id="weight_crystal" />, blank <ItemLink id="checkpoint" />s and a little gold.
- <ItemLink id="salvaged_model" />: see [Salvaged Models](salvage.md).
