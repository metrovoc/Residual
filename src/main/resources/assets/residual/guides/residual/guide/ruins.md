# Ruins

The old world left three kinds of building behind. They stand in grassland, forest, taiga, snow, savanna, desert,
hills, cherry groves and badlands, far apart: it takes travel to find them.

## Archive houses

Small houses of calcite and tuff, one corner usually fallen in. They kept the archive. Look in the chest and the barrels,
and brush the suspicious gravel where the corner came down.

## Relay masts

Tall copper masts with an old relay still on top and a chest at the foot. The old world talked across distances with
them; their chests hold relay cards and the plans for relays.

## Buried vaults

A lantern on a post beside a trapdoor in the ground. Below is a deep room with a bank of old accelerators, and two
chests. Every vault holds a [salvaged mind](salvage.md) and the plans for the most advanced machines.
