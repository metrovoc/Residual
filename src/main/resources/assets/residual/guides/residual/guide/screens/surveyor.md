# The Surveyor's screen

Use a Surveyor to open it. [The Surveyor](../surveyor.md) tells what its model reads.

<residual:Clip name="survey_screen" alt="A Surveyor counting what its model finds" />

With no model, it says what it needs; with one, the finds are counted here.

<residual:Shot name="surveyor_waiting" alt="A Surveyor with no model yet">
  <residual:Part name="model">
    **Model**: A model practised on the Survey Dataset. It reads the ground a row at a time and says where each ore and cache lies; what it gets right shows to anyone near holding a Survey Rod.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="finds">
    **What it found**: Each ore and the caches, with how many its model has found this survey. Point at one for more.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="progress">
    **How far it has gone**: Along the display's foot, how far this survey has gone. Point at it for how far it reaches.
  </residual:Part>
</residual:Shot>
