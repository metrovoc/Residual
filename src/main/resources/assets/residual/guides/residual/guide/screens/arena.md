# The Arena's screen

Use an Arena to open it. [The Arena](../arena.md) tells how a mob is written down and what a model has to do to play it.

With no log or model yet, it says what it needs.

<residual:Shot name="arena_waiting" alt="An Arena with nothing in it yet">
  <residual:Part name="log">
    **The log**: A Mob Log that has written down mobs of its kind. A base at the Frontier tier can also put a Surveyor's Field Log here, for ground.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="model">
    **Model**: A checkpoint, or a Relay Card tuned to a Model Server. Its model plays the mob in every fight, and how often it picks what the mob really did, on the tenth of the log kept from practice, is what the fights are worth.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="fights">
    **The mob**: The mob of the log. Along its foot a mark for each tier of likeness the model has reached; point at it for how the model did, what that earns, and what is in the way.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="drops">
    **What it won**: What the fights that count have dropped. A hopper can take it from here.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="power">
    **Power**: What it has stored, filling from the bottom. Point at it for what a fight costs.
  </residual:Part>
</residual:Shot>
