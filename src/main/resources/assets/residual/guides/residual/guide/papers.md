# Papers

Every lesson retraces a paper. They are all worth reading in full, and after the lessons you know enough to follow
their main argument.

## The path

- [Counting Pairs](lessons/counting.md): Claude E. Shannon. *A Mathematical Theory of Communication.* Bell System
  Technical Journal, 1948.
- [More Context](lessons/context.md): Yoshua Bengio, Réjean Ducharme, Pascal Vincent and Christian Jauvin. *A Neural
  Probabilistic Language Model.* Journal of Machine Learning Research, 2003.
- [Looking Things Up](lessons/recall.md): Dzmitry Bahdanau, Kyunghyun Cho and Yoshua Bengio. *Neural Machine
  Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate.* 2014, ICLR 2015.
- [Knowing Where](lessons/reverse.md): Ashish Vaswani et al. *Attention Is All You Need.* NeurIPS 2017.
- [Induction Heads](lessons/induction.md): Nelson Elhage et al. *A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits.*
  Transformer Circuits Thread, 2021.
- [Induction Heads](lessons/induction.md): Catherine Olsson et al. *In-context Learning and Induction Heads.*
  Transformer Circuits Thread, 2022.
- [Language](lessons/language.md): Alec Radford, Jeffrey Wu, Rewon Child, David Luan, Dario Amodei and Ilya
  Sutskever. *Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners.* OpenAI, 2019.

## The methods inside the Core

- Diederik P. Kingma and Jimmy Ba. *Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization.* ICLR 2015.
- Ilya Loshchilov and Frank Hutter. *Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization.* ICLR 2019. This is AdamW, the optimizer
  every lesson trains with.
- Razvan Pascanu, Tomas Mikolov and Yoshua Bengio. *On the Difficulty of Training Recurrent Neural Networks.* ICML
  2013. Gradient clipping.

## The machines

- Kaiming He, Xiangyu Zhang, Shaoqing Ren and Jian Sun. *Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition.* CVPR 2016.
  Residual connections.
- Jimmy Lei Ba, Jamie Ryan Kiros and Geoffrey E. Hinton. *Layer Normalization.* 2016.
- Biao Zhang and Rico Sennrich. *Root Mean Square Layer Normalization.* NeurIPS 2019.
- Dan Hendrycks and Kevin Gimpel. *Gaussian Error Linear Units (GELUs).* 2016.
- Noam Shazeer. *GLU Variants Improve Transformer.* 2020. Gated feed-forward layers.
- Jianlin Su et al. *RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding.* 2021. RoPE.

## Further

- Sepp Hochreiter and Jürgen Schmidhuber. *Long Short-Term Memory.* Neural Computation, 1997. The recurrent networks
  attention replaced.
- Mor Geva, Roei Schuster, Jonathan Berant and Omer Levy. *Transformer Feed-Forward Layers Are Key-Value Memories.*
  EMNLP 2021.
- Kevin Meng, David Bau, Alex Andonian and Yonatan Belinkov. *Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT.*
  NeurIPS 2022.
- Hugo Touvron et al. *LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Language Models.* 2023. RMSNorm, SwiGLU and RoPE together.
- Bo Peng et al. *RWKV: Reinventing RNNs for the Transformer Era.* 2023.
