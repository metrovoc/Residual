# The Watch Post's screen

Use a Watch Post to open it. [The Watch Post](../watch.md) tells what it watches for.

A row for each thing it watches.

<residual:Shot name="watch_post_watching" alt="A Watch Post watching a furnace">
  <residual:Part name="rows">
    **What it watches**: Each thing's line of looks as bars, newest on the right, red while something is wrong with it. Point at a row to hear what it thinks.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="model">
    **Model**: A checkpoint, or a Relay Card tuned to a Model Server, whose model passes Watch. It reads each thing's line, and what surprises it is what has gone wrong. Without one, or on a line it cannot follow, the Watch Post only sees what has stopped for two minutes.
  </residual:Part>
</residual:Shot>
