# An Assembler's screen

Use an Assembler to open it. [Storage](../storage.md) tells how a network crafts.

A recipe drawn in its grid.

<residual:Shot name="assembler_recipe" alt="An Assembler with a recipe for sticks">
  <residual:Part name="grid">
    **The recipe**: Click with a stack to put its kind in the recipe; click empty-handed to clear. The grid holds pictures, not items: the Assembler takes its ingredients from its network.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="result">
    **What it makes**: Worked out from the grid.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="keep">
    **Keep**: The four buttons beside the panel change how many to keep in the network; point at one to see the current number. It makes more whenever there are fewer. With nothing to keep, it makes only what is asked of it. With shift, sixty-four times as many.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="status">
    **What it is doing**: Point at the work arrow to see whether it is crafting, has enough, lacks something, or has no recipe yet.
  </residual:Part>
</residual:Shot>
