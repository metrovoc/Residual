# The Archive

The old world wrote letters, journals, stories, songs, recipes, manuals and notices, and kept a copy of everything.
What survives is in <ItemLink id="archive_shard" />s, each a piece of one record with some of its text lost.

## Shards

A shard learns which record it is the first time you hold it. Use it to read what is left; lost text shows as ▒.

## Archive drives

<RecipeFor id="archive_drive" />

Use an <ItemLink id="archive_drive" /> to file every shard you carry into it. Shards of the same record make one better
copy: whatever any of them kept is kept. The drive's tooltip counts its records and its legible letters.

In a Model Core's **Data** slots, a drive is practice for **Reading**: the mind learns to predict the next letter of
everything legible on it, so the more you recover, the more it has to learn from. Reading is tested on records no
shard ever carries. A mind can only pass by learning how the old world wrote, not by remembering what it read.

## The Reader

<RecipeFor id="reader" />

A <ItemLink id="reader" /> restores lost text. Put a shard in its tray and a mind in its mind slot: a checkpoint, or a
Relay Card tuned to a Model Server for a mind too big to hold. The Reader goes through the lost blocks one by one. For
each, the mind reads the text just before the block and then guesses the block letter by letter, seeing each true
letter after its guess. If it guesses at least half of them, the block was predictable enough to restore, and it
comes back.

A block the mind cannot restore stays lost until a better mind tries. A mind that reads ahead, seeing the letter it is
asked to guess, is turned away: it would only copy.
