# Salvaged Models

<ItemImage id="salvaged_model" scale="2" float="left" />

Every buried vault holds a <ItemLink id="salvaged_model" />: the record of a model the old world built and could not
make work. It keeps the model's machines, what it practised on and how it was trained. Each failed the way models often
do.

<br clear="all" />

## Rebuilding

Put a salvaged model in a Model Core's **Data** slot and press **Rebuild** on the **Data** tab. Its machines are built in
front of the Core as they were found: they need clear space, and they drop nothing when broken. Its training settings
come with it.

Train it as it is first, and watch how it fails. The loss curve, the **Inspect** tab and the machines themselves all
have something to say.

## Repairing

Find what is wrong and put it right: change a machine's settings, add or replace a machine, or change how it trains.
A salvaged model has a budget of 1,500 steps per run to reach its goal; when a run runs out, **Reset** its weights and
try again. A repaired model is worth experience, and what it took to repair it is worth more.
