# Flight

The <ItemLink id="glider" /> is a pair of wings of thin casing, worn in the chest slot. It glides as an elytra does:
jump while falling to spread it, and a firework rocket drives it on. It wears as it flies, and mends with
<ItemLink id="tensor_casing" /> on an anvil. The Fabricator makes it from a transformer photomask, as it makes
Forecaster II and the Smelter.
