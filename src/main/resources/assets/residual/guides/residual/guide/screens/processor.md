# A Processor's screen

Use a Processor to open it. [Storage](../storage.md) tells how a network crafts.

A pattern: what goes in, the machine in front, what comes out.

<residual:Shot name="processor_pattern" alt="A Processor's pattern: raw iron in, an ingot out, with a furnace in front">
  <residual:Part name="in">
    **What goes in**: What the machine takes for one round. Click with a stack to put that many there, right-click for one more; empty-handed, a click clears the cell and a right-click takes one off. The grid holds pictures, not items.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="out">
    **What comes out**: What the machine makes in one round. The first thing here is what the network can ask for; the rest come along with it. Click with a stack to put that many there, right-click for one more; empty-handed, a click clears the cell and a right-click takes one off.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="machine">
    **The machine**: What stands in front of the Processor, which it runs the pattern on.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="in:on">
    **Pages**: Nine pages of nine cells each way.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="keep">
    **Keep**: The four buttons beside the panel change how many to keep in the network; point at one to see the current number. It makes more whenever there are fewer. With shift, sixty-four times as many.
  </residual:Part>
  <residual:Part name="status">
    **What it is doing**: Point at the work arrow to see its rounds a second, or what it lacks.
  </residual:Part>
</residual:Shot>
