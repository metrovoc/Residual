Residual

Someone lived here before you. They built machines that thought, and they wrote everything down. Then they were gone. Pieces of what they left still turn up in the ruins: broken tablets of their writing, the designs of their machines, and models that no longer work.

These are field notes on building models again: language models made of machines that each do one operation, cabled into a network, trained on text, and tested on text they have never seen. A good one can farm, keep a storeroom, run errands, carry on a pattern, tell what a thing is by its name, understand what you tell it, read what the old world wrote, and see where a field is going.
